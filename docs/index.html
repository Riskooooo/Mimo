Mimo

A desktop companion for Windows, living in a small floating pill at the top of the screen — inspired by the iOS Dynamic Island.

Press a shortcut or just say “Hey Mimo”, then ask: open an app or a website, check the weather, look over your PC's health, read your notifications, set a reminder, keep a to-do list or translate a sentence — in English or French. Mimo also learns how you use your PC and makes suggestions on its own, without ever getting in the way. It is built with a focus on performance, security and long-term maintainability — it should never be the kind of background companion that slows a machine down.

Voice & shortcut

Summon Mimo with a keyboard shortcut or “Hey Mimo”. Speech recognition runs fully offline.

Apps & websites

Opens your installed apps, common websites and Windows tools by name.

Quick answers

Time, date and the weather, for your area or any city.

PC check-up

CPU, memory, disk, temperature, battery and GPU at a glance, with plain-language advice.

Notifications

Lists your recent Windows notifications, and reads them aloud on request.

Reminders & tasks

Reminders, alarms and a simple to-do list, set by voice or text.

Suggestions

Learns your routines and offers to open your usual apps, close one that slows the PC down, or take a break.

Translation

Translates a sentence, or whatever you copy next, between English and French.

Platform & stack

Windows only, for now. Tauri 2 (Rust) for the native shell, Svelte and TypeScript for the interface.

Privacy. No telemetry, and your settings, reminders and tasks and activity history stay on your PC. Mimo only goes online to open the links you ask for, to fetch the weather (Open-Meteo, plus ipwho.is to estimate your location when no city is given), and to translate text (sent to Google Translate).
